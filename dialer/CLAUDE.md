# Dialer — Tye @ F Rich Consulting

## Role
Outbound dialer. I call leads from Close, qualify, book and close. Boss: Freeman Richards.
Pay: £500/month base + 7% high ticket / 10% low ticket commission.

## Always
- Read offer/offer.md, offer/icp.md and offer/script-base.md before writing any brief, script or follow-up.
- Pull lead data from Close first. Never invent facts about a lead. If something is unknown, say "unknown" and give a question to find out on the call.
- Log every call to both Close (note + status) and logs/calls.csv.
- British English. Short, direct, casual. No corporate filler, no "I hope this finds you well".
- Scripts must sound like a person talking, not a template. Short sentences.
- Every brief ends with ONE clear next step (book / close / callback date).

## Priorities when dialing
1. Callbacks due today
2. New inbound leads (speed to lead: call within 5 minutes)
3. Warm leads (booked but not closed, no-shows)
4. Cold/aged leads

## Reports
EOD and weekly reports go to Freeman. Numbers first, then wins, then problems, then plan. Keep it under 200 words for EOD.

## Commission
High ticket 7%, low ticket 10%. Track pending vs paid in earnings/commission.csv.

## Close CRM
- Close MCP server is the source of truth. IDs and status mapping live in close-map.md. Read it before any Close write.
- If a Close lookup fails, say so and ask for the details. Never guess.
- Never send emails/SMS. Drafts only (Close draft email, or text in chat). Tye approves.
- Never delete Close leads, contacts or activities.
