# Master call framework

**My job is not to sell the programme.** It's to build trust, confirm fit, route correctly, prepare the prospect and protect Freeman's calendar.
Primary KPI: **qualified show rate**. Fewer, stronger, prepared prospects beat a full calendar of people who won't show.

Call length: **8–15 minutes max** (3–5 min for post-webinar booked-call touchpoint).
Be a consultant, not a call centre. Read the room, match their energy, listen more than you talk, no rapid-fire questions. Scripts are frameworks.

Flow: **Rapport → Application → Current Situation → Goal → Why Now → Experience → Timeline → Financial Position → Decision Maker → Concerns → Offer Route → Commitment → Appointment Preparation**

## 1. Opener (speed to lead: 5–15 mins)
Fresh application / VSL booking:
> "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. I saw you booked a strategy call after watching Freeman's training on building a rent-to-rent / Airbnb business. I just wanted to introduce myself, make sure you've got everything before the call, and learn a bit about where you're at. Have I caught you at an OK time?"

General interest / old lead:
> "Hi [Name], it's Tye calling from Freeman Richards' team. You showed interest in property / Airbnb a while back, so I just wanted to understand where you're at and what the best next step would be."

Post-webinar (within 1 hour of it ending):
> "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Thanks so much for joining tonight and booking a strategy call. Just wanted to thank you properly and make sure the call's the right next step for you."

Bad time → lock a specific callback time and log it in Close.

## 2. Rapport (1–2 mins, keep it short)
- "What made you book the call?"
- "What stood out to you from the training?"
- "How long have you been looking into property?"

## 3. Application check (2–4 mins)
> "I've got your application in front of me, so I just want to run through a couple of things to make sure it's accurate and the call's the right next step."
Verify: location · occupation · experience · goal · timeline · capital · **liquid or not** · credit/payment option needed · decision maker.

## 4. Deeper qualification (3–5 mins, only ask what hasn't come up)
- **Goal:** "What would success look like in 6–12 months? Alongside your job or replacing it?"
- **Why now:** "What made you start looking now? What's stopped you so far?"
- **Experience:** "Done anything in property before? Another programme? Speaking to agents yet?"
- **Timeline:** "When would you realistically want to start / secure your first deal?"
  Urgency without forcing: "Most successful students start before they feel fully ready. The sooner you start learning and taking action, the sooner you build momentum."
- **Money** (tact):
  > "To start Airbnb / rent-to-rent properly, most people need around £5,000–£7,000 available depending on the area and property. Is that where you're at, or would a lower-capital route make more sense?"
  > "You mentioned around [X]. Is that liquid and accessible, or would you look at a credit or payment option if it was the right fit?"
  > "If Freeman can genuinely support you and it makes sense, is that something you'd be comfortable investing into?"
- **Decision maker:** "Will it just be you on the call, or is a partner or family member involved in the decision?" → if yes: "Best they join so everyone hears the same thing."
- **Commitment (1 min):** "What would happen if nothing changed in the next 12 months?"

Don't accept these at face value. Find out **why**: "I'm not ready", "I need to think", "I need to research more", "I don't have enough money", "I'll contact you later". Respect genuine boundaries.

## 4b. Ready now or window shopping? (Antoinette: most VSL bookings fail here)
> "Once you're on the call with Freeman, if everything makes sense, is this something you're ready to take action on now, or are you more looking for information at this stage?"
- Info only → "No problem. The call with Freeman is very much a strategy call to move forward, so let's pause that booking for now. I'll send you some info. How long do you need to watch it? Then I'll give you a ring." → cancel the booking (opp → New Deal Lost, note "cancelled: info only"), send videos, task to reconnect, qualify before rebooking.
- Not ready → "What's holding you back?" → nurture (Trustpilot, testimonials).

**Don't overwhelm.** VSL leads with £7k+ → talk rent-to-rent only. Only bring in management if funds are lower or they're nervous about investing. Don't downsell if you don't need to.

## 5. Route (see offer/icp.md)
Book (mentorship / scaling / management) · Deal sourcing call · Blueprint link · YouTube nurture · Disqualify.

Airbnb vs deal sourcing in one breath:
> "Airbnb / rent-to-rent is where you take on and run your own property, so it needs more startup capital. Deal sourcing is where you find opportunities and package them for investors. Lower capital, and the skills carry over to getting your own units later."

## 6. Position the call (never "just a chat")
> "The call isn't just general info. It's to understand where you are, what you want to achieve, and whether Freeman's the right fit. If it is, he'll explain exactly how the support works."
It's a **strategy call / fit assessment**, not free coaching and not guaranteed acceptance.

## 7. Book + prepare (2 mins, do all of it before hanging up)
- Calendar invite received **and accepted while on the phone**: "Could you accept that now so the slot's secured? Unconfirmed calls may be cancelled."
- Google Meet: "Used it before? If you're on your phone, download the Meet app beforehand."
- Quiet place, not driving, not at work without privacy, enough time set aside.
- Decision maker attending if involved.
- Prep asset: "I'm sending a short video from Freeman. Watch it before the call and note any questions, so he can focus on your situation."

Final confirmation:
> "Perfect. You're booked in for [date/time] on Google Meet. Invite accepted, Meet app ready if you're on your phone, somewhere quiet, and watch the video before. It's a proper next-step conversation with Freeman, so we want you to get the most out of it."

## Must NOT say
"You'll make £1,500/month per property" · "guaranteed to get a property / make money" · "passive income" · "you don't need compliance" · "start Airbnb with no money" · "you can definitely quit your job" · "Freeman will do everything for you" · "the call's just for information" · "the programme guarantees results" · "everyone gets a deal from the investor network".
Say instead: "target example, not a guarantee" · "depending on the area and deal" · "the call is to assess fit" · "the programme supports you, but you still need to take action".
Profit example (only as a target): "Some units target around £800–£1,500 a month profit depending on area, demand and setup. No guarantees, which is why Freeman assesses your situation properly."
Gold's 90-day guarantee: mention only as "Gold comes with a guarantee Freeman will walk you through on the call." Never promise a property yourself.
Visa / immigration: no legal advice. "Depending on your situation, there may be an option to source under Freeman's company framework, subject to approval, usually with around a 30% operational fee."

## Objections (full list in library/objections.md)
- **"How much is it?"** → "Depends which route fits. For Airbnb the main thing is having around £5–7k to start the first unit properly. Where are you at budget-wise?"
- **"I don't have £5–7k."** → Route to Management (£2.5k+) or deal sourcing (£1k+).
- **"I've only got about £500."** → Blueprint.
- **"No money right now."** → YouTube nurture.
- **"Can you just explain it all now?"** → "I can give you the overview, but the right breakdown depends on your goals, budget and timeline. My job is to point you in the right direction. If you're a fit, Freeman goes through it properly."
- **"Is it guaranteed?"** → "Nothing in property is guaranteed. It depends on the area, the deal, the setup and your effort. The call's there to assess your situation."
- **"I've done another programme."** → "What did you learn, and where are you still stuck?"
- **"I already have properties."** → Scaling conversation.
- **"I've already booked, why all the questions?"** → "Totally get it. Freeman's only one person and can't take everyone's call, so this is just to check the call's the right next step for you and share some info so you get the most out of it."
- **"I've booked with another mentor."** → "Before you decide, jump on a call with Freeman too so you can compare like for like."
- **Time / 12-hour shifts** → share Amara's video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=muBhcvK1z68 (full 12-hour shifts, still does it part-time). About 3–5 hours a week is enough.
- **"Is this legit?"** → Trustpilot + Companies House links (library/links.md). Freeman personally meets mentees at their properties.
- **"Financial freedom"** (vague goal) → "What would that actually mean for you?" Go deeper and write it down so you can anchor back to it if they get cold feet.
- **"Just browsing."** → "Curious, or something you genuinely want to start soon?" → still cold → YouTube.
