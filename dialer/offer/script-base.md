# Master call framework

Goal on every connect: book the call with Freeman (or close if it's my deal). Fallback: a callback with a set day and time.
Tone: calm, friendly, curious. Short sentences. Let them talk. Aim for them talking 60%+.

## 1. Opener (15 sec)
> "Hi [name], it's Tye from Freeman Richards' team. You filled in the form about getting into Airbnb, rent-to-rent. Have I caught you at an OK time?"

New inbound (under 5 mins):
> "You literally just filled in the form, so I thought I'd ring while it's fresh. Got two minutes?"

Bad time → "No worries. When's better, later today or tomorrow morning?" Lock a time and hang up.

## 2. Discovery (the bulk of the call)
Use their form answers. Don't re-ask what they told us — confirm and dig.
1. **Where they are:** "You said you're [Q1]. What've you done so far?"
2. **What they want:** "In the next 90 days you want [Q2]. Why that, why now?"
3. **The dream:** "You wrote [Q6]. What does that actually look like day to day?" → go one level deeper: "And why does that matter to you?"
4. **What's stopping them:** "You said [Q8]. How long's that been holding you back?"
5. **Work situation:** "You're [Q3]. How many hours a week could you realistically put in?"
6. **Money:** "On the form you said [Q10]. Is that ready to go if the right thing's in front of you?" If "under £5k": "Roughly how much, ballpark?"
7. **Decision:** "If this made sense, is it just you deciding or anyone else?"

## 3. Pitch (keep it short, use their words)
> "So from what you've said, you want [goal] because [why], and the thing stopping you is [blocker]. That's exactly what Freeman does. It's 1-to-1, weekly calls plus WhatsApp access, and he walks you through finding the property, the agents, the contracts, getting it live. Same systems he uses in his own business."

Product steer:
- £2.5k–£5k → Airbnb Management / Co-hosting (lower entry, no lease).
- £5k+ and wants speed → 3-Month Fast Track.
- £5k+ and wants more support or is worried about risk → 6-Month Gold with the 90-Day Guarantee.
Proof: "I'll send you the Trustpilot and a couple of the mentee case studies so you can see who he's helped."

## 4. Close (book)
> "Best next step is a call with Freeman. He'll look at your situation and map out exactly what you'd do first. I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which works?"

Then: confirm number and email, tell them what to have ready (funding figure, any areas they're looking at), send confirmation text straight away.

Fallback: "No problem. Let's put 10 minutes in for [day/time] and pick it up then." Always a date and time. Never "I'll give you a ring sometime".

## Common objections — short answers (full list in library/objections.md)
- **"I need to think about it."** → "Fair. What is it you want to think over? The money, the time, or whether it'll work for you?"
- **"I haven't got the money."** → "You put [Q10] on the form. Has that changed?" If under £2.5k → DQ politely.
- **"I'm too busy with work."** → "Most people Freeman works with have a full-time job. How many hours could you give it a week?"
- **"Can I just learn it on YouTube?"** → "You could. The difference is someone checking your deals and contracts before you sign a lease. That's where people lose money."
- **"Does it actually work?"** → "Don't take my word. I'll send you the Trustpilot and the case studies now."
