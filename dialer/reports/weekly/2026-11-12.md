# Weekly update for Freeman: Thu 5 – Thu 12 Nov

Hi Freeman, here's how this week has gone.

It's been a busy week. I made about 360 dials and had around 25 proper conversations. We had roughly 40 new apps and booked 20 calls with you, and three people signed (Hector, Ryehan & Hamzah, and James). Overall the leads have been good. More of them are genuinely serious than in October. A few had already taken real steps before speaking to us, like setting up a limited company or going to other seminars, and the strongest ones came in with clear reasons: more time with family, getting out of shift work, or building something for their kids.

Most of the people applying are in full-time work, a lot of them in healthcare, care or other shift jobs, and many are parents fitting this around a busy week. Most are complete beginners. Money-wise, most apps come in at under £5k, or £3–5k with credit, so the majority are routing to Management. A steady group sit at £5–7k for rent-to-rent, with a handful at £7–10k+. The figure on the form is often higher than what they actually have spare once we talk it through, so I'm checking the real number on every call now.

The objections that keep coming up are trust and past bad experiences with other courses or mentors (a few have been burnt by hard-sell events or group programmes), time around shifts and kids, the partner not being on the call, and wanting "time to think" after your call. Reviews and a case study sent before your call seem to help a lot with the trust side. The main problem this week has been show rate. A number of self-bookers are hard to reach before their slot, and a few pulled out by text or didn't turn up even after confirming.

To tighten that up, I'm making sure everyone has a pre-call by the night before or we free up the slot. They confirm by reply on the day. I ask how sure they are out of 10 so I can deal with any doubts before they get to you. Everyone gets the reviews and a case study beforehand. I've also set your Airtable to update every hour until 10pm with notes and the call outcome, so you can see where everyone's at.

Thanks, Tye
