# Next call plan (Close note format)

A short prep note on the lead before every call, so Tye always knows what to say. **Freeman can see Close notes**, so it reads as professional call prep, not a script:
- No "SCRIPT" headings.
- Suggested wording stays short and natural. One line per step, in quotes, only where it helps.
- No internal commentary: no commission maths, no criticism of past texts or of anyone on the team, no "say this to get them".
- Facts come only from Close (notes, calls, SMS, application answers). Unknown = "unknown", plus the question that finds it out.
- Follows Freeman's VSL flow (library/vsl-script-freeman.md): no payment plans or prices, ask location (Scotland = no R2R), video + "one rule", provisional booking.

## Title
`Next call plan (D Mon)`. Add a short tag if it matters: `Next call plan (2 Oct): client check-in`.

## Body (plain text, these headings, in this order)

```
AIM
One or two lines: why we're calling and what a good outcome is.

WHERE THEY'RE AT
* Source + when (VSL app / webinar / old lead) and status
* Work · based · who's involved
* Goals (their words)
* Funds (liquid / credit)
* What's happened so far (last contact, what was sent, what they said)
* Still unknown: ...

APPROACH
Tone + one or two things to be mindful of (e.g. nervous, prefers text, sensitive personal situation).

CONVERSATION FLOW
1. Reconnect: one natural opening line
2. Check-in on what's changed / the video
3. Their situation + goals (the questions still unanswered)
4. Money: confirm from what they've said before
5. Why now
6. Next step: book Freeman / check-in / date

LIKELY CONCERNS
* Concern → how to handle it (one line each, Freeman's challenge style)

NEXT STEP
Book Freeman + video + check-in, OR a dated follow-up, OR NGMI/DQ. Plus what to text if there's no answer.
```

Keep it to a screen or so. It should be readable in 60 seconds before dialling.
