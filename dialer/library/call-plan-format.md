# Next call plan (Close note format)

A short prep note on the lead before every call, so Tye always knows what to say. **Freeman can see Close notes**, so it reads as professional call prep, not a script:
- No "SCRIPT" headings.
- Suggested wording stays short and natural. One line per step, in quotes, only where it helps.
- No internal commentary: no commission maths, no criticism of past texts or of anyone on the team, no "say this to get them".
- Facts come only from Close (notes, calls, SMS, application answers). Unknown = "unknown", plus the question that finds it out.
- Follows Freeman's VSL flow (library/vsl-script-freeman.md): no payment plans or prices, ask location (Scotland = no R2R), video + "one rule", provisional booking.

## Title
`Next call plan (D Mon)`. Add a short tag if it matters: `Next call plan (2 Oct): client check-in`.

## Body (plain text, these headings, in this order)

```
AIM
One or two lines: why we're calling and what a good outcome is.

WHERE THEY'RE AT
* Source + when (VSL app / webinar / old lead) and status
* Work · based · who's involved
* Goals (their words)
* Why / pain / why now (their words, or "unknown, dig on this call")
* Funds (liquid / credit)
* What's happened so far (last contact, what was sent, what they said)
* Still unknown: ...

APPROACH
Tone + one or two things to be mindful of (e.g. nervous, prefers text, sensitive personal situation).

CONVERSATION FLOW
1. Reconnect: one natural opening line
2. Check-in on what's changed / the video
3. Their situation + goals (the questions still unanswered)
4. The why: 2–3 "why" questions written for this lead (see below)
5. Money: confirm from what they've said before
6. Why now: play their why back in one line before booking
7. Next step: book Freeman / check-in, on a specific day and time + confirmation SMS

LIKELY CONCERNS
* Concern → how to handle it (one line each, Freeman's challenge style)

NEXT STEP
Book Freeman + video + check-in, OR a dated follow-up, OR NGMI/DQ.

IF NO ANSWER (text, Tye approves before sending)
One short, personal text that references something specific to them (their goal, what they said last time, the video). Ends with one easy question. Never more than one text per call attempt.
```

## The why (Freeman, 12 Oct): every plan has it
A call that only confirms logistics is a "receptionist" call. Every plan must have 2–3 why questions written for that lead, using what they've already said, from Freeman's ladder (library/vsl-script-freeman.md, "Freeman's coaching, 12 Oct"):
- "Why are you looking for a mentor?"
- "Why is now the right time?"
- "Why not just try it with [the alternative they mentioned: a friend's property, on your own, YouTube]?"
- "Why does [their goal] matter to you?" / "What happens if nothing changes?"
If their why is already known, quote it and plan to use it to book. If it isn't, write "Why: unknown" in WHERE THEY'RE AT. No clear why = don't book Freeman yet.

## Video check-in plans (after they've watched the video)
The check-in is where the why gets locked in. The flow is:
1. How did you find the video? What stood out? Why do you think Freeman's a good fit for you?
2. **At least three why questions** from the ladder, tailored to them (e.g. "You said you're tired of 12-hour shifts. Why is now the time to change that, not next year?" / "Why not just try it with your cousin's flat on your own?").
3. Play it back: "So you want [goal] because [pain], and now's the time because [reason]. Right?"
4. Then excitement, partner, and confirm the Freeman slot (or book it). Set expectations: it's a plan for their situation, not a price.
In LIKELY CONCERNS, include the flat "it looks good" answer → dig for the real why before confirming.

## No-answer text: building blocks
Pick the one that fits, then personalise the [bracket] with something from their history:
- **First attempt, new application:** "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Just tried you about your application to work with Freeman. When's a good time for a quick 5-min chat?"
- **Following up on the video:** "Hi [Name], Tye from Freeman's team. Just tried you. Did you get a chance to watch the video? Keen to hear what you thought, and what made you think it could work for you. When suits for a quick call?"
- **Agreed check-back (they said 'in a few months'):** "Hi [Name], Tye from Freeman's team. You mentioned [checking back around now / after your travels / once things settled]. Is Airbnb still on the cards? When's good for 5 mins?"
- **Warm lead gone quiet:** "Hi [Name], Tye from Freeman's team. Last time you said [their goal, in their words]. Still the plan? I've got Freeman's diary open this week if you'd like to get a call in."
- **No-show:** "Hi [Name], Tye from Freeman's team. We missed you for your call with Freeman. No stress at all, these things happen. Want me to find you another time that suits?"
- **Final attempt (after 3+ tries):** "Hi [Name], Tye from Freeman's team. I don't want to keep chasing, so I'll leave it with you. If Airbnb's still something you want to do, just reply here and I'll call when it suits you."


Keep it to a screen or so. It should be readable in 60 seconds before dialling.
