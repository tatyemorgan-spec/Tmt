## 1. VSL leads: they watched Freeman's training and applied/booked

Goal: 8–15 min. Confirm fit, route, prepare, lock the show. Call within 5 mins, 15 max.

**Opener**
> "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. I saw you booked a call after watching Freeman's training on building an Airbnb / rent-to-rent business. I just wanted to introduce myself, make sure you're all set for the call, and get to know where you're at a bit. Have I caught you at an OK time?"

**Rapport (1–2 mins)**
> "What made you book in?"
> "What stood out to you from the training?"
> "How long have you been looking at property?"

**Application check (2–4 mins)**
> "I've got your application here. I'll just run through a couple of bits so we know the call's the right next step for you."
Confirm: where they live, job, experience, goal, timeline.

**Money (with tact)**
> "You put around [Q10] on the form. Is that sitting there ready, or would you look at a payment plan or credit if it was the right fit?"
> "And if Freeman can genuinely help and it makes sense, is that something you'd be comfortable investing in?"
If "under £5k": "Roughly how much, ballpark?" → route (see icp.md).

**Go one level deeper (this is what makes them show up)**
> "You wrote [Q6 dream]. What does that actually look like for you day to day?"
> "Why now? What's changed?"
> "What's stopped you starting before?"
> "If nothing changed in the next 12 months, how would you feel?"

**Decision maker**
> "Is it just you deciding, or is a partner involved? If they are, best they join so you both hear the same thing."

**Position the call**
> "Just so you know, it's not a general chat. Freeman looks at your situation, what you want, and whether he can actually help. If it's a fit, he'll show you exactly how."

**Lock the show (don't hang up until all done)**
> "Can you see the invite? Could you accept it now while I'm on, so the slot's secured?"
> "It's on Google Meet. If you're on your phone, download the Meet app before."
> "Make sure you're somewhere quiet, not driving or at work."
> "I'm sending you a short video from Freeman. Watch it before the call so he can focus on you, not the basics."
> "Perfect. So that's [day/time], invite accepted, Meet ready, quiet spot, video watched. Looking forward to hearing how it goes."

**No answer:** call → voicemail → text → log → task.
> Voicemail: "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Saw you booked a call, just wanted to confirm the details and send over some prep. I'll drop you a text now."

**Then:** confirmation text + video straight away → 24h reminder → morning-of → 1–2h before.

## 2. Webinar leads

### a) Booked a call on the webinar: call within 1 hour of it ending (3–5 mins)
> "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Thanks so much for staying on tonight and booking in. I just wanted to thank you properly and make sure the call's the right next step."
> "What stood out to you from the webinar?"
> "What made you want to book?"
Then the light check: goal, timeline, money (liquid?), comfortable investing, decision maker, understands it's a next-step call → send video → lock the show (same as VSL).
No answer → voicemail + text + try next working day:
> "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Saw you booked in after tonight's webinar. Just want to confirm your call and send some prep. I'll text you now and try you again tomorrow."

### b) Attended but didn't book (5–8 mins)
> "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. Saw you joined Freeman's webinar on [topic]. Did you get value from it?"
> "Was there any reason you didn't book a call at the end?"
> "Anything you were unsure about?"
> "Are you still keen on getting support with [rent-to-rent / Airbnb]?"
If yes → light qualify → only book if they're serious:
> "Sounds like a call could be useful. I only want to book it in if you're serious about taking action and happy to watch a short video before. Does that sound fair?"

### c) Registered but didn't attend
> "Hey [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. You signed up for Freeman's webinar on [topic]. Did you manage to make it?"
> No → "No worries. You missed a good one, he broke down [topic]. I'll send you the training. Are you still interested in how [rent-to-rent / Airbnb] could work for you?"
If yes → light qualify → book or route.

Order the day after a webinar: calls today → unconfirmed bookings → booked but not reached → attended no-book → registered no-show → nurture.
Whole list worked within 3 working days (2–3 attempts + a text each).

## 3. Questions to ask the previous dialler

### Money (so you get paid on everything you're owed)
1. How is a close credited to me? Do I get commission on any close from a call I booked, or only ones I close myself?
2. How are Ray and I split if we both touched the lead?
3. When is commission paid: on signing, or as each instalment lands? What happens if they refund?
4. Do I earn on Deal Sourcing, Blueprint or Management sales? At what rate?
5. What did you actually earn in a good month, and what did that month look like (bookings, shows, closes)?

### The offer right now
6. What are the current prices and payment options for each route: Mentorship, Management, Deal Sourcing, Blueprint?
7. What's the exact wording for Gold's 90-day guarantee, and when can I mention it?
8. Where do I get the prep videos, case studies, Blueprint link and YouTube links to send?
9. Which assets actually get watched and lead to shows?

### Leads
10. Where does each lead come from (VSL, webinar, IG, Calendly) and which source books and closes best?
11. How many fresh applications come in a day or week, and when do they usually land?
12. Which old lead lists are worth working? Any gold in the old webinar or Calendly leads?
13. What do the Close statuses really mean day to day? What's the difference between Setter Pipeline and Closer Follow Up?
14. How do Slack new-app claims work with Ray? First to ✅ wins?

### Calls
15. What are the top 3 objections you hear, and what do you say that actually works?
16. What makes a lead no-show, and what have you done that fixed it?
17. What's your show rate and your booked-to-close rate?
18. What's the best time of day to reach people?
19. Which leads should I never book? Any red flags Freeman hates seeing on his calendar?
20. Can I listen to 3 of your best calls and 1 bad one?

### Freeman
21. What does Freeman want to see in the notes before a call?
22. What annoys him most: no-shows, weak leads, messy notes?
23. When is his calendar busiest? Should I leave gaps?

## 4. How to earn more in this role

- **Commission comes from closes, and closes come from shows.** A booking that doesn't show earns £0. Invite accepted on the phone + video sent + reminders is where your money is.
- **Book the right people.** Freeman closes qualified, prepared leads. Weak bookings drag his close rate and your pay down.
- **Speed to lead.** Call fresh applications within 5 minutes. The chance of reaching someone drops off fast after that.
- **Don't throw leads away. Route them.** £2.5–5k → Management (10% on £2.5–3k = £250–£300). If you earn on Deal Sourcing, a £1–2.5k lead is money too.
- **Recover no-shows.** 40%+ recovery is basically free bookings.
- **Build the follow-up bank.** "Not now" leads with a dated task become next month's bookings.
- **Track it.** /eod and /earnings show bookings → shows → closes → £. Know your numbers before Freeman asks.

Maths: £2k/month = £500 base + ~£1,500 commission ≈ 5–6 closes. At a 25% booked-to-close rate that's ~22–24 shows, so ~28–30 bookings at 80% show rate, about 1.3–1.5 bookings a working day.
