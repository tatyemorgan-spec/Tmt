# How Freeman runs a closing call (analysis of two calls)

Two of Freeman's recorded calls, broken down so I know (1) the flow he follows, (2) the lines that do the work, and (3) what he needs from me before the call so he can close.

- **Mary Adeola, 12 Oct (45 mins).** Rent-to-rent mentorship. Lecturer in Birmingham, two kids back home, about £6–7k (my figure, which Freeman quoted back to her). **Closed on the call** (instalments, first session booked, WhatsApp group made).
- **Ben & Sienna, 6 Oct (47 mins).** Ray's lead. Deal sourcing programme (lower ticket). Couple in Essex, shift workers, 2–3 months into property. **Closed on the call** (paid during the call, onboarded into the community, 90-day plan sent).

Both calls follow the same spine. Prices and payment plans are Freeman's part only. I never say them.

---

## The flow (both calls)

| # | Stage | What Freeman does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rapport** | Opens on something personal and local: "My first ever property was in Birmingham, B17, Harborne." With Ben: "You went travelling, didn't you?" | 1 min |
| 2 | **Context check** | Confirms what I've done: "You've applied… you've had conversations with Tye… he sent you a few videos… you've watched them?" If they haven't finished: "What did you get up to?" | 1 min |
| 3 | **Frame the call** | "This call isn't a consultation, it's not a chit-chat. It's for me to understand where you are now, where you want to be. If I can genuinely help, we look at the mentorship, the investment and the process. And if you're happy, we're going to get you out of your job and start making money. That's my agenda. Does that sound like a plan?" | 1 min |
| 4 | **Where they are now** | "What have we done so far? Courses, programmes, webinars? Give me the juice." "How long has this interest been with us?" "Why property?" "What would you say has ultimately stopped you from turning this interest into an actual business?" | 2–3 mins |
| 5 | **Work and pain** | "How long have you been doing that? Is it something you enjoy, something you want to do for the next 10, 20, 30 years?" Lets them say it: "I can't continue like this forever." | 2 mins |
| 6 | **Goal with numbers** | "What type of mentor would I be if I didn't know where you wanted to go?" Then: "6 to 12 months from now… how much money, how many properties, what position would you need to be in to say this mentorship paid off?" | 2 mins |
| 7 | **Raise a low goal** | Mary said £1–1.5k a month. Freeman: "Honestly, that's a very humble goal. We can triple that." Anchors £3–4k with proof (first property in 6–7 weeks, £500–1k profit per property). He never accepts a small goal. | 1 min |
| 8 | **Future pace** | "Let's put ourselves in that version of you making £3k a month. What does your life look like? What's different? What are you doing now?" | 2 mins |
| 9 | **Personal why** | "Outside of yourself… family, partner, kids?" Then affirms: "Your goal is bigger than you. It's selfless. That's why you're going to be so successful." | 2 mins |
| 10 | **Timeline** | "When are we actually looking to start? Are we in research mode, or if I find the right mentor it's go time?" | 1 min |
| 11 | **Dig into their words** | Mary said "a real mentor". Freeman: "You said a *real* mentor. What characteristics would convince you?" (Answer: results. He then shows results.) | 1 min |
| 12 | **The why challenge** | "Why are you even looking for a mentor? It's not free. You're a lecturer, a very intelligent woman. Why not ChatGPT, Google, YouTube? Why are you on a call with me?" Ben and Sienna got the same: "With all the free info online, why aren't you just learning this yourself? Why are you on a call with me?" **They sell themselves**: accountability, misinformation online, someone who's done it. | 2 mins |
| 13 | **Hero and guide** | (Ben call) "It's your movie, you're the hero. I'm just the guide. But there's nothing worse than trying to help someone who doesn't want to help themselves." They answered: "I'm willing to put in the work." | 1 min |
| 14 | **Model + objection** | Re-explains the model in one line and checks: "Does that concept make sense?" Handles "What if there's no one booking?" by tying it to the due diligence pillar. | 2 mins |
| 15 | **Confirm money** | Uses my figure: "Tye mentioned you had about £6–7k… and that's cash, liquid, that you're happy to invest?" | 30 secs |
| 16 | **Present the programme** | In pillars (setup and compliance, due diligence, marketability, scaling). Ties each one to something they said ("this is the part of the video you didn't watch, so you'd have answered your own question"). Checks "Make sense?" after each. | 8–10 mins |
| 17 | **Proof, live** | Shares his screen: his booking platform, real bookings, monthly numbers. Does the maths with them: "How many properties would you need to match your salary?" She says 3–4. "Money provides options." His own story (homeless when they arrived, mum a nurse, dad a bus driver). "I'm not showing you this to show off." "Can you picture it? Then it's already happened." | 5–8 mins |
| 18 | **Alignment check** | "How does that align with what you're looking for in a mentor?" When she says "it aligns": "**How?**" Makes her say it in her words. | 1 min |
| 19 | **Commitment** | "To be successful you need about 3–4 hours a week. Is that manageable?" "If you follow instructions… I don't see why you won't have one or two properties by the end of the year." | 1 min |
| 20 | **Isolate before price** | "Before I get into the investment, is there anything that would stop you or prevent you from moving forward?" ("Not at the moment.") | 30 secs |
| 21 | **Student proof before price** | Screenshots of student wins, chosen to match her: busy professionals, a single mum, Victoria in Scotland travelling 4 hours by bus. "No excuses." | 3 mins |
| 22 | **Investment + options** | (His part only.) Gives the options, lets them choose. Ben and Sienna asked if it covers both of them; he said yes as one unit, and added a free 1:1 "because I really want to see you guys win". | 2 mins |
| 23 | **Assumptive next steps** | "Do you want to know what the next steps look like?" Books the first session, takes her number, "add me to your emergency contacts", sends the payment link while on the call, invites her to the Tuesday community call, makes the WhatsApp group. | 5 mins |
| 24 | **Handle friction calmly** | Card declined (Ben): "No worries, banks are funny", fills the wait with the 90-day plan and small talk, then retries. Mary needed to move money: he booked **me** to call her after 4pm to finish it. | as needed |

## The lines worth stealing (for my calls)

- "This call isn't a consultation, it's not a chit-chat. It's the next step." (Already in my script. Freeman says it the same way.)
- "Give me the juice." "How long has this interest been with us?" "What would you say has ultimately stopped you?"
- "What type of mentor would I be if I didn't know where you wanted to go?" (Swap to: "What kind of help would I be if I didn't know where you wanted to get to?")
- "Why are you even looking for a mentor? There's YouTube, Google, ChatGPT. Why are you on a call with us?"
- "You said [their word]. What does that mean to you?" (He always digs into the exact word they used.)
- "How does that line up with what you're looking for?" … "How?"
- "Can you picture it?"
- "Is there anything that would stop you from moving forward?" (Isolate before the big step.)
- Affirm every answer: "110%", "I love that", "that's really, really good."

## What Freeman needs from me before his call (the handoff)

From both calls, this is what he relied on (or had to fix himself):

| Freeman needed | On Mary's call | My job |
|---|---|---|
| **Video watched** | She'd only watched part of it, so he had to re-explain the model and the bookings objection. | Enforce the one rule. If it's not watched, the check-in isn't done. |
| **Capital confirmed** | He quoted my figure and moved on in 30 seconds. | Always confirm the exact liquid figure and put it in the Notes for Freeman. |
| **Their why** | He spent ~10 mins getting the pain, goal and family reason. | Get it first (Freeman, 12 Oct) and write it in their words, so he can go straight to future-pacing. |
| **Their goal in numbers** | He had to get it and raise it. | Ask "how much a month would make this worth it?" and note it. |
| **Who decides** | Mary: alone. Ben: both on the call. | Partner on the call, or a clear "I decide". |
| **Their exact words** | He leaned on "real mentor" and "every money I made is from my sweat". | Quote their phrases in the notes. |
| **Likely objection** | "What if there are no bookings?" | Note the objection they raised with me so he can pre-empt it. |
| **After the call** | He handed Mary back to me to chase the payment after 4pm. | Same-day follow-up on anything left open. |

**Notes for Freeman (before every call):** funds (exact, liquid), why in their words, goal in numbers, work and pain, decision-maker, video watched (yes/partly/no), the objection they raised, anything sensitive.

## My calls vs his calls

My job isn't to close. It's to do stages 2–12 well enough that Freeman can skip to 14. The why challenge (stage 12) and the goal with numbers (stages 6–7) are where my calls have been weakest (Kenzie). If I do them, the lead turns up to Freeman already invested.
