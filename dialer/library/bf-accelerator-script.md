## The call (5–8 mins)

### 1. Opener (warm lead)
> "Hey [name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. You got a sec? Last time we spoke you were keen on [their goal from the notes], but [timing / budget / the 1:1] didn't quite land. Freeman's just put something together for people in exactly that spot. Can I take five minutes to see if it fits?"

**Opener for a new webinar sign-up:**
> "Hey [name], Tye from Freeman's team. You registered for Sunday's Airbnb session. Have you got five minutes so we can point you at the right bit?"

### 2. Reconnect (use their own words)
- "What's changed since we last spoke?"
- "Is getting that first property still something you want? Why's it still on your mind?"
- If their why has gone cold, warm it up before moving on: "What happens if nothing changes by next summer?"

### 3. The two money questions (separately)
1. "Realistically, how much could you put towards learning this properly?"
2. "And after that, what would you have left to actually start, for deposits and setup?"

Cash, finance and credit are different things. Never push borrowing.

### 4. Time and the decision
- "Could you give 3–5 hours a week on top of the live sessions?"
- "Anyone else who'd need to agree? Would they want to be on the call?"

### 5. Curiosity before the details
> "So here's what's different. Instead of the full 1:1, it's a 12-week live programme. You learn with a small group and implement as you go, and you get proper support doing it. It starts 7 December, with a break over Christmas. There are three ways in, depending on how much hands-on help you want. Want me to tell you which one I think suits you?"

### 6. Recommend ONE tier, with a reason from their answers
| They said | Recommend | Why, in their words |
|---|---|---|
| Keen but tight budget, happy to learn in a group | **Academy** | "You get the full teaching and group support without stretching yourself." |
| Wanted Freeman, worried about doing it alone, or burnt by a group course before | **Accelerator** | "You get a 30-min strategy session with Freeman, a small private group with Megan and a progress check, so you're not left on your own." |
| Good funds, wants deal flow, in an area we can service | **VIP** | "Same support plus five verified landlord introductions in your area." |

**VIP:** only in areas we can service. The introductions are conversations with interested owners, never guaranteed properties.

### 7. Price and close (only after Monday's sign-off)
> "Academy's £797, Accelerator's £1,297 and VIP is £2,497, all paid upfront. Checkout is in US dollars through Skool, so your bank shows the dollar amount. Based on what you've told me, I'd go with [tier] because [reason]. How does that sound? What would you need cleared up before deciding?"

- **Ready:** "If the structure, price and dates work, I'll send the official checkout and terms now. You're enrolled once the payment goes through." Not Won until it's paid.
- **Big question or partner involved:** "Let's get you 25 minutes with Freeman so you can ask him directly. There's no pressure to commit." Then send the 25-min booked text.
- **Hesitant:** never ask "yes or no today?". Ask "What specifically is still unresolved?" and book one next step.
- **Monthly options:** only quote ones that are live at checkout. Never make up a plan.

## Objections (diagnose first)
- **"I've already been through all this with Freeman."**
  > "Totally, and this isn't the same thing. The 1:1 was the full package. This is a group route with a smaller way in, for people who want to start now."
- **"Still can't afford it."**
  > "What's your full budget, including setup?"

  If it doesn't stretch to Academy plus a buffer, nurture them. Don't push.
- **"I can learn it on YouTube."**
  > "You can, and a lot of it's out there. What you don't get is someone checking your deals and keeping you accountable week to week."
- **"Bad course before."**
  - Say exactly what's live and personal (Accelerator: the Freeman session and Megan) versus group.
  - Send reviews and the timetable.
- **"Will I get a property?"**
  > "Nobody can promise that. What I can tell you is exactly what support you get."
- **"Why USD?"**
  > "Skool bills in dollars. Your card may add a small FX fee. I'll give you the rough pound figure."
