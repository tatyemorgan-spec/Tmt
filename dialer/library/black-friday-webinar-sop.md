## The setter flow
1. **Opt-in:** record the source and contact permission, and send the webinar assets.
2. **Call:** ask for 5–8 minutes. Log whether I reached them and when.
3. **Qualify:** the result they want, their starting point, money (**two separate questions**: what's available for the mentorship, and what's left to start the business), 3–5 hours a week, who else decides, and what they need to feel confident.
4. **Recommend a tier:** explain all three tiers and the full prices, then give my recommendation with a reason.
5. **Next step:**
   - **5A (ready and suitable):** explain the terms, send the official checkout, and confirm the payment has landed. **It isn't Won until paid.**
   - **5B (qualified but unsure):** book a 25-minute call with Freeman, with the decision-maker on it.
   - **5C (not a fit):** let them down kindly, send the free resources and nurture or DQ them with the real reason.
6. **Follow-up:** one next action, one owner, and no double chasing.

**What I can and can't do as setter:**
- I can explain the approved terms and send the secure checkout to a ready, informed buyer.
- I can't negotiate fees, approve instalments, give guarantees, add VIP areas, or answer legal, visa or tax questions. Those go to Freeman.
- Never put card details in Close, WhatsApp, SMS or a note.

**If they hesitate:** never ask "yes or no today?". Ask "What's still unresolved?" and agree one next step.

## Objections (diagnose first)
- **Too expensive / I need the money for the property:** get their full startup budget. Never push borrowing. If they can't afford it, nurture them.
- **I can learn it for free:** agree, then explain what guided implementation and feedback add.
- **I've been burnt by a course before:** say exactly what's live and personal and what's group. Show real proof, the written terms and the timetable.
- **Can you guarantee a property?:** no. VIP guarantees the introduction service only, under the written criteria.
- **Will 5 leads get me one deal?:** outcomes vary. No conversion rate is promised.
- **My partner decides:** set up a joint call.
- **Management or R2R?:** check the cash left after tuition and their location. Book Freeman if it's still unclear.
- **Can I pay in 3 or 4 parts?:** only quote checkout options that are enabled.
- **Why is it in USD?:** explain the USD billing and give the indicative GBP figure.

## Reminders
- **After booking:** calendar invite with the timezone and link, one pre-call asset, the one-page tier summary, and notes in Close.
- **Day before:** one confirmation, and confirm the partner is joining.
- **On the day:** a morning reminder, or a 1–2 hour reminder if they haven't confirmed. Use judgment rather than sending every reminder.
- **No-show:** one call and one reschedule text. Mark the no-show honestly.
- **They say no:** log the real reason and stop chasing.

**Handover to Freeman (10 lines max):**
- Name
- Budget for the mentorship, and the separate budget for setup
- Model preference
- Goal and timeline
- Time available
- Partner / decision-maker
- What I've sent them
- Tier and price acknowledged
- Main objection
- Next question or next action

## Tracking
- **Log in Close:**
  - Lead source (webinar, warm Close, organic, VSL) and ad ID if there is one
  - First-contact time and attempts
  - Tier recommended, GBP price and USD checkout amount
  - Booking, show or no-show
  - Cash collected and date, future instalments, refunds
  - Owner and next action
- **Keep these separate:** Signed Up, Balance Owed, and any unrelated lost opportunity.

- **Rules:** paid on cash collected, adjusted for refunds, never paid twice. Any split between setters must be agreed in advance.
