# VSL call flow: for confidence (not a word-for-word script)

**Your only job on this call: find out 5 things, then decide the next step.** You're not selling. You're *sorting*: is this person right for Freeman's call or not? If you forget a line, it doesn't matter. Just get the 5 things.

## The 5 things (write these on a sticky note)
1. **Why now?**
2. **Goal:** in their words
3. **Funds:** how much, ready or not
4. **Who decides:** alone or with someone
5. **Ready:** act now, or just looking?

## The flow (8–12 min)

**1. Open (say this word for word, then breathe)**
> "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman Richards' team. You watched the Airbnb video and filled in the application, have I caught you at an OK time?"

Bad time → "No worries, when's better, later today or tomorrow morning?" → lock a time → done.

**2. Hand it to them (then stop talking)**
> "I've got your application here. I just wanted to get a better idea of where you're at, what's got you interested in property and why now?"

→ **Listen.** Nod, take notes. They'll often answer 2–3 of your 5 things on their own.

**3. Fill the gaps (only ask what they haven't told you)**
- Goal: "And what would that actually mean for you, if it worked?"
- Funds: "On the form you put [X]. Is that ready to go, or would you look at a payment plan if it was right?"
- Who decides: "Is this just you, or is someone doing it with you?"
- Ready: "If it all makes sense on the call with Freeman, is this something you'd want to act on now, or more looking for info at this stage?"

**4. Decide + next step**
| What you heard | Say |
|---|---|
| £5k+, ready, clear why | "Sounds like a call with Freeman makes sense. He'll map out exactly what your first steps look like. I'll send you a short video to watch first. When works for you, and [partner] if they're involved?" → book → Call Confirmation text |
| £3–5k / nervous about money | "There's a lower-entry route, Airbnb management, where you don't take on a lease. I'll send you a quick video on it. Can I ring you [day] to hear what you think?" |
| Wants info / not ready | "No problem at all. Freeman's call is a strategy call to move forward, so let's not rush it. I'll send you a video, and give it a watch. When should I ring you back?" |
| Under ~£2.5k / no plan | Friendly, no pressure. YouTube channel, door left open → DQ email |

**5. Close the call**
> "Perfect. I'll send that over now. Speak [day]."
Then straight away: `/log [name] [outcome] [notes]`.

## When you freeze (keep these handy)
- "Tell me a bit more about that."
- "What made you look into it now, rather than a year ago?"
- "And how would that change things for you?"
- "Fair enough. What's holding you back at the moment?"
- Silence is fine. Count to 3 before you speak. People fill the gap.

## Confidence
- **You know the offer. The call isn't a test of that.** You only need their 5 answers.
- **Curious, not perfect.** Asking "why?" with genuine interest beats any script.
- **Warm up first.** Make your first call of the day an easy one (a callback or a warm lead), not your best fresh lead.
- **Stand up and smile** while you talk. It sounds warmer and calmer.
- **Notes as you go** in the 5 boxes, so you always know what's left to ask.
- **Bad call? Doesn't matter.** Log it, next one. Confidence comes from reps, not from getting each one perfect.
