# Five-minute first call (Freeman, 30 Oct)

Freeman's new flow for the first call on a new app. It overrides the long qualifying flow for first calls. The aim is to get the vital info in about 5 minutes, route them, and let the training video (the sales asset) do most of the talking. The why ladder, the partner question and the Freeman booking move to the check-in after they've watched the video.

Every new call plan builds its CONVERSATION FLOW from these 8 steps, filled in from their application (use their answers, in their words). Go by the question, not the Q number, since funnels differ.

## The flow

1. **Open + why they applied.** "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman Richards' team. You applied to work with Freeman on the one-to-one Airbnb mentorship, right? What made you want to do that?" Let them talk. If it's vague, one follow-up from their dream-life answer: "You wrote [Q6]. What does that look like for you?"
2. **Frame.** "I'm just going to go through your application to make sure you're the right fit for the one-to-one paid mentorship. It'll take a few minutes. Is that OK?" (Under £5k with no credit: use the unqualified line from new-app-flow.md 2c here instead.)
3. **Journey (Q1).** "You said you're [their journey answer]. Have you done anything in property yet: any courses, programmes, or do you own property?"
4. **Intent + timeline (how soon).** "Assuming Freeman can support you in starting, scaling and growing your Airbnb business, the investment's in line with your budget, and he can help you hit your goals, would you be open to moving quite quickly with this?" Then: "Are you shopping around at the moment?" Play back their how-soon answer if it helps ("You put ASAP").
5. **Work + location (work situation).** "You said you're [their work answer]. Give me a bit of context on that." Then: "And where are you based?" (Scotland = no rent-to-rent.)
6. **Funds (funding).** "On the application you said [their range]. Just so we can link you to the best strategy, what have you set aside comfortably to invest in this? Is it closer to [low end] or [high end]? And what does credit look like?"
7. **Route.** Name the strategy that fits and explain it in a line or two, then ask "How does that provisionally sound?" Give the rough start-up figure for that strategy, including the mentorship, out loud only. Never write a figure in the note.
   - ASA2 £7k+ → rent-to-rent only. Never mention Management.
   - ASA2 £5–7k → rent-to-rent.
   - ASA2 under £5k (or "about 3k plus credit") → "I know you came in on rent-to-rent, but I think Management will be a better strategy for you."
   - ATA1 → Management, whatever the funds.
   - Scotland → no rent-to-rent.
8. **Hand to the video.** "If you'd like to explore what working with Freeman looks like, and you're committed to this, I can send you a free training video that properly explains it. If it sounds like something you want to do, we can touch base [day/time they pick] and I'll see if the mentorship's a good fit. How does that sound?" Send ONE video for the route, and set the check-in task at the time they agree.

## What moves to the check-in (after the video)
The why ladder (library/vsl-coaching-notes.md, 12 Oct), the decision maker on the call, the spoken commitment, then the Freeman booking. Use the video check-in flow in library/call-plan-format.md.

## Self-booked leads (Booked Call)
Same 8 steps. At step 8, instead of offering to touch base, confirm their Freeman slot, send the video, tell them the one rule, and set a check-in before the call.

## In the note
- Fill each step with their own answers, so Tye can read it straight off.
- Keep LIKELY CONCERNS to the two or three objections their answers point to (work situation, how soon, blocker), one line each.
- No prices, payment plans, instalments or discounts anywhere.
