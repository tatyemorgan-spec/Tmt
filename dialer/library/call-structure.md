## 1. The call: a conversation, not a questionnaire (8–15 min)
Keep the checklist pinned and glance at it, but let *them* talk. People who open up answer half your questions before you ask them.

| Stage | What you're finding out | Easy way in |
|---|---|---|
| Open (30s) | Permission + reason | "Hi [Name], it's Tye from Freeman's team. You applied after watching the Airbnb video. Got a few minutes?" |
| Open it up (2–3 min) | Why property, why now | "I just wanted to get a better idea of where you're at, what's got you interested in property and why now." Then **shut up and listen**. |
| Their story (2–3 min) | Research history, other mentors, job, location, family | "How long have you been looking into it? Done any courses or webinars?" / "What do you do for work at the moment?" |
| Goal + pain (2 min) | The real why | "What would that actually mean for you?" Go one level deeper and write down their words. |
| Money (1–2 min) | Funds, liquid, credit | "You put [X] on the form. Is that ready to go, or would you look at a payment plan if it was right?" |
| Who decides (30s) | Partner / friend / family | "Is this just you, or is someone doing it with you?" (Lurelle: doing it with a friend, so Freeman's call was moved so he could join.) |
| Ready or window shopping (30s) | Commitment | "If it all makes sense on the call with Freeman, is this something you're ready to act on now, or more looking for info?" |
| Route (1 min) | Which offer | £5k+ → rent-to-rent (don't overwhelm). £3–5k / nervous → Management (Tolulope). |
| Next step (1–2 min) | Book / videos / callback | Book + confirm, or send videos + set a callback day (Tolulope: "call back Friday"). |

## 2. The note: copy Antoinette's format
Bullet points, bold the bits Freeman needs. Written straight after the call.

```
* [One-line headline: Great convo / Not ready / etc.]
* [Age, family] - [how long researching, other webinars/mentors, paid or free]
* [Why they want mentorship]
* Based: [town]
* Work: [job, hours, WFH?] - [career goal]

* **Funds: [£ + liquid / credit]**
* Decision: [alone / with partner/friend + name]
* Knows about: [R2R / Management / both] - what you discussed

```

## 3. Texts (SMS / WhatsApp)
- Use their first name. One message = one ask ("when's good to call?").
- Short. Antoinette's templates are on the long side, so trim if it's a quick back-and-forth.
- Always say who you are: "Tye from Freeman's team".
- One text per call attempt. Never double-text without a call in between.
- Match the testimonial to them (library/links.md). Examples, not promises.
- **Paste WhatsApp chats into Close as a note.** If it's not in Close, it didn't happen.
