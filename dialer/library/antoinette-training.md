# Training from Antoinette (previous dialler), 25 Sep 2026

Source: Fathom recording "Impromptu Google Meet Meeting", 76 mins. Where this differs from the SOPs, **this is how the job actually runs**.

## Biggest takeaways
1. **Speed to lead, even out of hours.** If an app lands at night / weekend, send an acknowledgement SMS straight away ("thanks for taking the time to watch, I'll be in touch soon"), tick ✅ in #1-new-apps, set a task for the first acceptable call time (9–10am).
2. **Wait a few minutes after a new app lands.** Many then book a call. Close merges the booking into the same lead and it shows in Slack call notis. Check before you call so you know if they've booked.
3. **Booked calls are the urgent ones.** Non-booked apps matter, but a booked call means videos need to go out and fit needs checking before Freeman's slot.
4. **Most VSL booked calls are info-seekers.** Her last batch: most had to be cancelled. Ask: "Is this something you're ready to take action on now, or are you looking for information?" If info only → cancel the booking, send the videos, agree when you'll reconnect, qualify, *then* rebook. Protect Freeman's diary even when it hurts your numbers.
5. **Don't overwhelm.** Freeman's rule for VSL leads: £7k+ → talk **rent-to-rent only**, don't pitch management too. (She used to explain both; Dorca got overwhelmed and hung up.) Management only when funds are lower or they're nervous about investing. Don't downsell if you don't have to. *Open question: confirm with Freeman how to handle the middle bracket.*
6. **Webinar leads are the best leads.** They sat through 2 hours and knew they needed £6–8k. VSL leads are often side-hustle seekers who need educating and often ghost once they see the work involved.
7. **Webinar leads book a discovery call with YOU, not Freeman.** 20–30 min video call (camera on). Then you book them with Freeman.
8. **Go deeper on pain points** and anchor back to them when they get cold feet. "Financial freedom" → "What would that mean to you?"
9. **Don't over-remind.** Freeman wants morning / afternoon / 5 mins before, but people complained about being chased. Her rhythm: morning SMS + a call later (or vice versa), then an SMS ~15 mins before: "check in early and make sure the link works".
10. **If it's not in Close, it didn't happen**, including WhatsApp chats (paste them into a note).

## VSL lead process (step by step)
1. New app in #1-new-apps → ✅ claim → open the Close link.
2. Give it a few minutes: have they booked a call too?
3. Out of hours → acknowledgement SMS + task for the next acceptable time.
4. Call. No answer → **VSL follow-up 1** SMS ("tried you about your application, just a quick call to understand your situation and goals and see how Freeman can best support you, let me know when I can call") + task (later today / tomorrow).
5. Next attempt. No answer → **VSL follow-up 2** SMS ("have I got the right person?") + task next day.
6. Still nothing → **last message**: "I've reached out several times. If this isn't for you, no worries. If you are interested, let me know and we'll pick up where we left off." + task next day to check.
7. No response → **NGMI**. At least 3 points of contact, door always left open.
8. Wrong number? Email them asking for an alternative number.

## Connected call: what she covers
Where they're based · current work · why property, why now · why pay for mentorship when it's free online · experience · goals · funds · **sole decision maker?** (try to get partner on Freeman's call; shift workers make it hard) · urgency: "Once you're on the call with Freeman, if everything makes sense, is this something you're ready to take action on now, or are you window shopping?"
- Not ready → "No problem. What's holding you back?" → nurture: Trustpilot, testimonials, keep in touch (other mentors are hitting their feeds too).
- Open it up naturally: "I just wanted to get a better idea of where you're at, what's got your interest in property and why now." Then let them talk and take notes.
- One-word-answer people: break the ice, crack a joke, keep asking open questions.
- She keeps the question list pinned and glances at it to check she's covered everything.

## Close pipeline rules (she was explicit)
- **Booked (VSL self-book):** lead shows as New Opportunity automatically.
- **Booked by you (webinar / qualified lead):** change lead status to **New Opportunity** AND **add an opportunity** at the bottom with the call **date**. If you skip the opportunity it won't show properly. (Webinar setters never did this, so it's new for you.)
- **Cancelled / no-show:** lead status → **Setter Pipeline** (or NGMI), opportunity → **New Deal Lost** with a note ("no show after confirming").
- Set a follow-up task (e.g. 2 weeks) on no-shows before leaving them.
- **Duplicate leads:** merge (check email/phone first). Note if funding answers differ between applications.

## Webinar lead process
1. Setters handle pre-webinar. Watch the webinar yourself (Freeman sometimes changes the pitch).
2. As soon as it ends: **phone** everyone who booked. Quick thank you + "I'll be in touch". No answer → acknowledgement SMS.
3. Day of discovery call: reminder SMS with the video link; laptop fine, phone needs the Meet app.
4. **Discovery video call** (20–30 mins, camera on, be friendly and chatty): "What did you think of the webinar? Biggest takeaway? Why now?" + all the VSL qualification questions. Notes titled "Discovery video call notes". Freeman doesn't want these recorded.
5. Qualified → **book them with Freeman** (+ create opportunity with date).
6. If Freeman's call is days away: send Trustpilot, testimonials, check-ins every 2–3 days. E.g. call on Monday → contact Friday with videos for the weekend → Monday morning check.
7. "Notes for Freeman" before his call.
8. After a sale: congratulate on WhatsApp (builds rapport for instalment follow-ups). **Set a task a few days before the next instalment**: "Call to see how she's enjoying the programme + remind about second payment."

## Objections, her way
| They say | She says |
|---|---|
| Time (12-hour shifts) | Share **Amara's** video: she does full 12-hour shifts and still makes it work part-time. You need about 3–5 hours a week; shift patterns often have days off. |
| Money (below threshold) | Encourage them to save and not give up on the dream. This business can break even quickly compared to most. If they've got ~£3k, management is an option. |
| "I've already booked, why are you asking all these questions?" | "Totally understand. Freeman's only one person and can't take everyone's call, so this is just to understand where you're at, check the call's the right next step, and share info so you get the most from it." |
| "I've booked with another mentor" | "Before you decide, get on a call with Freeman too so you can compare like for like." (She won one this way.) |
| 80% sure, cold feet | Anchor back to their pain points. Tell Freeman they're 80%, not 100%. |
Most objections show up *after* they watch the videos (they ghost), not on the call.

## Under £5k applicants
Don't write them off. Some have more than they say (e.g. family money) and people hide what they have. £3k → management is possible. Follow up 3 times, then NGMI.

## Setter Pipeline vs NGMI
She moves to NGMI after several attempts rather than keeping hundreds in Setter Pipeline. If they come back, put them back in Setter Pipeline. Freeman may disagree, so check.

## Lead flow
VSL leads vary a lot: some days 0, some days 3–5, often overnight. Relying on VSL alone for income can be stressful. Plan your day around follow-up tasks (she had ~19 a day).
