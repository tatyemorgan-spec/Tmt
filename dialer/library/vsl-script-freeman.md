# VSL call script: Freeman's way (from his role-play, 29 Sep)

**The mindset (Freeman's words):** "We only want PIFs. We don't want callbacks and follow-ups. The only way we get PIFs is people being as informed as possible. You are their best friend through the whole process. I only want to be moving forward. Minimal back and forth."
Freeman should **never** have to educate someone on his call. If they're not informed, qualified and keen, they don't go in his diary.

## Before you dial (1 min)
- Read all 10 application answers. Note: **funds, timeline, blocker, dream, work**.
- Have **Freeman's calendar open** with 2 slots in mind.
- Which video did they come from: rent-to-rent or **management**? Talk about *that* one. Don't switch them (an overqualified management lead stays on management).
- **Energy up before you dial.** Stand up. Keep it up to the end of the call, not just the start.

## The call

**1. Open**
> "Hey, is that [Name]? … Hey, it's Tye from Freeman's team, how you doing?"
> "I believe you filled out an application to work with Freeman, him supporting you on your Airbnb journey. I think you filled it out [yesterday evening]. Ring a bell?"

**2. Frame it (short + low pressure)**
> "Amazing. I've just got five minutes. I just want to make sure this might be a good fit for you, explain a bit about how we might be able to support you, and what the next steps could look like."

**3. The video + the model**
> "Thank you so much for filling out the application, I've got a lot of info about you here. There was a video on the page. Did you manage to give it a watch? Have you got a basic understanding of what the business model looks like?"
> "So you're familiar with rent-to-rent, where we take a property from a landlord and let it out night by night on Airbnb?"
*(Management lead: "…where you manage Airbnbs for property owners, no lease, no rent, no furniture.")*
Never say "VSL" to a lead. That's our word.

**4. How long + the hold-up**
> "How long have you been looking to get started in property?"
> "Just out of curiosity, what's been the hold-up? [A year] is a long time."

**5. Their situation + pain (go deeper here, it's the gap in Antoinette's calls)**
> "What do you do for work, if you don't mind me asking?"
> "Is that something you enjoy? Something you see yourself doing for the foreseeable, or are you looking for a change?"
> "What do you think would be the biggest change for you? Buying back your time, the money, or something else?"
Write their words down. You'll use them again.

**6. Where are they based** (Freeman: important)
> "And whereabouts are you based?"
Builds rapport ("we've helped someone near there"), and **Scotland doesn't work for rent-to-rent.**

**7. Who's involved**
> "With this business, is it just yourself, or have you got a life partner or business partner helping you out?"
If yes:
> "How serious is that conversation? With or without them, are you looking to go all in on this, or is it something you very much want to do together?"
Plant it: they should be willing to go ahead even if the partner isn't (Adeline was keen, her partner wasn't, and it fell apart).

**8. Money (use the application so it's not intrusive)**
> "I can see from the form you put [just under £5k] to get started. What sort of investment have you set aside comfortably, ballpark?"
If short for rent-to-rent:
> "You're slightly on the tighter side for rent-to-rent, if I'm honest. Have you got the luxury of any credit cards? Some business costs can go on credit, so you keep your cash for the big things like rent and deposit."
Confirm a number:
> "So shall we call it [£4k cash and £2k on credit]? Is that a safe amount you're happy to invest?"
**Don't mention payment plans.** That's Freeman's card to play on his call.

**9. Why now / why this time**
> "Out of curiosity, [Name], you've been looking at this for [a year], webinars, probably a hundred YouTube videos. Why is this time different? What are you looking for?"

**10. Book provisionally (move forward, no "I'll get back to you")**
> "I think you'd be a great fit for the programme. You've got a lot of the characteristics it takes to run this business, and I'm sure Freeman's going to take a real liking to you. I've got his calendar in front of me and he's got some availability this week. Does [the weekend] work at all?"
Fit it round their life:
> "You said you work nights. Would you rather do before work, or a few hours after you wake up?"
→ **Pencil the slot in Freeman's calendar** (remove the block if it falls through).

**11. The partner**
> "Are they aware of the application? Have you got their number? I'll make a quick WhatsApp group with the three of us, because I'm sure they'll have a few questions, and we can confirm [11am Saturday] works."

**12. Position the call**
> "I've provisionally blocked that in. I really want the call with Freeman to be the best use of your time, and for you to have all the info to make a decision, because **the call with Freeman isn't a consultation or a chit-chat. It's the next step.** Like you said, you want to take action."

**13. The asset + "one rule"**
> "I've got a free exclusive training video that goes deeper into the business, how to start your Airbnb, how to get it profitable, and how Freeman can help. Would you find that helpful? Do you think it'll help you make the decision?"
> "Amazing. I've just got **one rule**: before your call with Freeman, you watch it. It's about 25 minutes. When will you be able to watch it?"
Book the check-in **outside work hours** (not their lunch break):
> "Cool, so shall I give you a buzz [tomorrow at 3]? I'll give you a bit of homework and ask a few questions to make sure you really get it. You might say 'Tye, this isn't for me', or 'I love this, I'm excited for Saturday'. Either's fine. How does that sound?"

**14. Tease the proof**
> "Freeman's worked with a few other [shift workers] who've had amazing results. After the video, I'll send you some of their stories too."

## The check-in call (after they've watched)
> "How'd you find the video? What did you like about the business model? What did you like about Freeman? Why do you think he's a good fit for you? Does it all make sense?"
> "So how excited are you for Saturday?"
> "As promised, here's [client] who reminds me of you. I had this exact conversation with them this time last year: same position, a job they didn't like, long hours. They finally made the change. I want that to be you, [Name]."
Get the partner on. Check again: "Even without [partner], is this something you're willing to do?" and "When you start seeing results, what do you think [partner] is going to say?"

## Freeman's feedback on your first run (fix these)

| Said "VSL" | "the video on the page" |
| Didn't confirm they watched + understood the model | Step 3, every call |
| Took ages to get to money | Use the application: "I can see from the form you put X…" |
| Offered payment plans | Never. Freeman's card |
| Full sentences, a bit robotic | Short, casual, react to what they say |
| High energy at the start, faded | Keep it up to the last line |
| Check-in during their lunch break | After work / their free time |
| No provisional dates | Pencil a slot on the first call |
| Forgot location | Always ask (Scotland!) |
| One style for everyone | Adapt: basic and warm with an auntie, "bro" with a young guy |

## After the call
1. Pencil Freeman's calendar (remove the block if it's not happening).
2. WhatsApp group with the partner. Send the video.
3. Close task: check-in call at the agreed time.
4. Close note (headline, based, work, **Goals**, **Funds**, decision maker, video sent, provisional slot) → `/log`.
5. Airtable row.

## Daily practice
15-min role-play with Freeman every day until ads switch on. Objections at 4 stages: before applying → after applying → after sending the video → after Freeman's call. Use `/drill` for extra reps.
