# TikTok/IG Reels launch scripts — Coaching Offer

## Script 1 — Origin story hook (30–45s)
1. Hook: "I spent 2 years 'planning to start' before someone finally
   made me actually do it."
2. Tell the (adapted) story of the transformation your coaching
   creates — keep it about the *client*, not your credentials.
3. Bridge: "That's exactly why I started Momentum Coaching."
4. CTA: "Free 20-minute call, link in bio, zero pressure."

## Script 2 — Objection-handling (25s)
1. Hook: "'I don't need a coach, I just need to be more disciplined.'"
2. Reframe: "Discipline isn't the problem — nobody follows through
   alone, that's why every athlete has a coach even at the top of
   their sport."
3. CTA: "Free discovery call, link in bio."

## Script 3 — Social proof (20s)
Read a real (anonymized) testimonial on camera, then: "That's a real
client, 6 weeks in. If you want that, book the free call — link in
bio."

## Caption template
```
Two years of "planning to start" fixed in one free call.
No pitch, no pressure — just 20 minutes to see if this is a fit.
Link in bio 👇
#coaching #accountability #momentumcoaching #goalsetting
```

## Email nurture (for discovery-call no-shows / "let me think about it")
**Email 1 (same day):** short recap of what they said on the call + the
package you recommended + the booking link. No new pitch, just make it
easy to say yes.

**Email 2 (3 days later):** one specific tip related to their problem —
pure value, soft CTA at the bottom.

**Email 3 (7 days later):** "Still thinking it over? No pressure — the
[Sprint] spot is still open this month if you want it." Last touch,
then stop unless they engage.
