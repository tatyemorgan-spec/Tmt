# Discovery call script (20 minutes)

**Goal:** understand their real problem, establish that you can help,
make a clear offer — never pressure.

1. **Open (2 min):** "Tell me what's going on — what made you book this
   call?" Let them talk. Don't pitch yet.
2. **Dig (5 min):** "How long has this been a problem? What have you
   already tried?" You're listening for what *hasn't* worked, so your
   pitch doesn't repeat their past failed attempts.
3. **Reflect (2 min):** Play back what you heard in one sentence: "So
   it sounds like the real issue isn't [surface problem], it's
   [deeper pattern]. Does that land?"
4. **Bridge (3 min):** "Here's how I work with people in that exact
   spot…" — describe your process in plain terms, not features.
5. **Offer (3 min):** Present ONE recommended package (not a menu) —
   "Based on what you told me, I'd actually suggest [Sprint/Quarter/
   Circle] because [specific reason tied to what they said]."
6. **Close (3 min):** "Want to start this week, or do you want to think
   it over?" Both answers are fine — if they need to think, send a
   one-line follow-up email same day, not a hard sell.
7. **Next step (2 min):** If yes — send the payment link/booking link
   on the call, while you're both still talking, not "I'll send it
   later."

**Never say:** "So, do you have any questions for me?" as your closing
line — it hands control back with no direction. Always end with a
specific next step.
