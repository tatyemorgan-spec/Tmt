# TikTok/IG Reels launch scripts — POD Store

## Script 1 — "I launched a store with $0" (30s)
1. Hook: "I launched a merch store with $0 upfront and zero inventory."
2. Screen record: Printful mockup generator turning the SVG design
   into a real shirt photo.
3. Show the connected Shopify/Etsy checkout.
4. CTA: "Landing page + designs are free/linked below — link in bio."

## Script 2 — Explain the POD model (25s)
1. Hook: "You don't need $2,000 in inventory to sell shirts."
2. Explain print-on-demand in plain terms over b-roll of the storefront
   page.
3. CTA: "Starter kit link in bio if you want to skip the setup."

```
Launched a merch store with zero inventory and zero upfront cost 👕
Print-on-demand means it prints + ships only when someone orders.

#printondemand #merchstore #sidehustle #shopify
```
