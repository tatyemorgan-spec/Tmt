# 100 AI Prompts for Local Service Businesses

*A ready-to-use prompt library for salons, contractors, cleaners,
trainers, and every local business owner who needs marketing help but
doesn't have a marketing team.*

Copy a prompt, paste it into ChatGPT/Claude, fill in the `[bracketed]`
parts with your business details, and get a usable first draft in
seconds. Every prompt in this pack has been written to need **zero
prompt-engineering skill** — just fill and go.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile & Local SEO (10 prompts)

1. Write a Google Business Profile description for [business name], a
   [business type] in [city], under 750 characters, highlighting
   [top 3 services] and [what makes you different].
2. Write 5 Google Business Profile posts announcing [seasonal
   promotion/new service], each under 300 characters with a clear call
   to action.
3. Generate 10 relevant Google Business Profile categories for a
   [business type] — primary category first, then secondary options.
4. Write a response to this 5-star review: "[paste review]" — warm,
   specific, under 60 words, no generic phrases like "we appreciate
   your business."
5. Write a professional, de-escalating response to this negative
   review: "[paste review]" — acknowledge the issue, avoid admitting
   legal fault, invite them to contact you directly.
6. List 15 local SEO keywords a [business type] in [city] should target,
   split into "high intent / ready to book" and "research phase."
7. Write meta title and meta description tags (under 60/155 characters)
   for the homepage of a [business type] website targeting [city].
8. Write FAQ content (6 questions) for a [business type] website that
   targets common "near me" search questions.
9. Suggest 5 photo categories [business type] should upload to Google
   Business Profile to maximize local ranking, with a one-line shot
   list for each.
10. Write a "Why choose us" section (120 words) for [business name]
    comparing against generic competitors, without naming any
    competitor directly.
