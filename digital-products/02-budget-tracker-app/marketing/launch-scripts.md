# TikTok/IG Reels launch scripts — Budget Tracker

## Script 1 — "I made my own budgeting app" (30s)
1. Hook: "I was paying $9.99/month for a budget app so I built my own
   instead."
2. Screen record: add a $42.50 grocery expense live, watch the chart
   update instantly.
3. Show the budget goal bar turning red on an over-budget category.
4. CTA: "It's $15, one-time, link in bio."

## Script 2 — Privacy angle (25s)
1. Hook: "Budget apps sell your spending data. Mine can't — there's no
   server."
2. Quick screen record of exporting a CSV, then closing wifi/airplane
   mode and showing the app still works.
3. CTA overlay: "Your money, your device, nobody else's business."
