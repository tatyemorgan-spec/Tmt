# Digital Products

Seven real, tested digital products — the "Top 7 AI digital products to
sell in 2026" categories, each actually built rather than described:
a Notion template, a working budget-tracker app, an AI prompt pack, a
social template pack, a coaching offer + booking site, a mini-course,
and a print-on-demand storefront.

**Start here:** [`MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md`](./MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md) —
pricing, bundling strategy, a 14-day launch calendar, and what still
needs a real account on your end (Gumroad/Etsy/Stripe/Printful) versus
what's already finished here.

Every product folder has its own `README.md` (what it is, how it was
built, how to customize it, honest notes on what needs your own
accounts) and a `marketing/` subfolder (listing copy, launch scripts
for TikTok/IG, and any product-specific sales assets).

Products #5 and #6 both lean on [`../site-builder`](../site-builder/),
this repo's static-site generator — proof the same tool that builds
local-business websites works just as well as the foundation for a
coaching sales page or a course delivery page.
