# Mini Course — "Get Your First Local Website Client in 14 Days"

A real 5-module course, delivered as a single self-contained HTML page
(sidebar nav, no login, works offline once downloaded). It teaches the
exact workflow this repo enables: find local business prospects, build
them a real sample site with `../../site-builder`, send outreach that
gets replies, price and close the deal, then turn one client into
referrals.

This is the most strategically important product in the pack — it's
not just a sellable course, it's also the onboarding document for
anyone using this repo to actually build the "sell local business
websites" business the rest of these products support (the Notion
Life OS for running the business, the AI Prompt Pack for client
marketing, the Canva templates for client social kits, the coaching
offer as a template for productizing any service).

## Contents

Each of the 5 modules has real instructional content plus an
interactive (checkbox) action-step checklist:

1. Find 20 Real Prospects
2. Build a Sample Site in Under 30 Minutes
3. The Outreach Message That Gets Replies
4. Price & Close Without Being Salesy
5. Turn One Client Into Five

Verified in a headless browser: all 5 modules render, all 15 checklist
checkboxes are present and interactive, zero console errors.
