# TikTok/IG Reels launch scripts — Mini Course

## Script 1 — Results-first hook (30s)
1. Hook: "I got my first $500 website client with one DM. Here's
   exactly what it said."
2. Read the outreach template from Module 3 on screen.
3. Show the before/after of the sample site that made the pitch land.
4. CTA: "Full 5-step system is $27, link in bio."

## Script 2 — Myth-busting (25s)
1. Hook: "You don't need to cold-call 100 businesses to get your first
   client."
2. "You need 3 finished samples and one good message." (list them
   fast: prospects → sample → outreach → price → referral)
3. CTA: "Whole system, step by step, link in bio."
